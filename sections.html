Header group

Shown at the top of every page. In the editor it's the Header group at the top of the sidebar.

Announcement bar

Short messages on a strip of tape above the header.

Color scheme
The bar's colors. The demo uses the dark Scheme 6.
Use moving tape Checkbox · on
Moves the messages along the tape. Messages that fit on one line stay still.
Speed Slow · Normal · Fast
How fast the tape moves.

Announcement block (up to 12)

Text
The message.
Link
Makes the whole message a link. Links inside the text are then shown as plain text.
Open link in new window
Opens the link in a new browser tab.

Only add messages that are true for your store. Replace the demo messages with your own.

Header

Your logo or store name, the main menu, search, the account icon and the cart. The logo itself is set in Theme settings → Logo.

Color scheme
The header's colors.
Enable sticky header Checkbox · on
Keeps the header in view while customers scroll. After scrolling, only the logo row stays visible.
Menu
The menu for the header and the mobile menu. Up to three levels show.
Menu style Ticket strip · Mega menu
Mega menu shows every link of a dropdown at once in a wide panel. The mobile menu always uses the ticket strip. See Menus.
Customer account menu
The links in the account menu that opens from the account icon.

Menu promo block

An image, heading and text at the end of one dropdown, and under that item in the mobile menu.

Menu item
Type the exact title of a top-level menu item that has a dropdown. Capital letters and spaces at the ends don't matter. One promo per item: if two blocks name the same item, the first one shows.
Image
Shown at its own ratio.
Heading
The promo's heading. A promo without an image, heading or text is hidden.
Text
A short line under the heading.
Link
The heading links here, and the whole promo becomes clickable. Needs a heading.

Footer group

Shown at the bottom of every page. The Footer section holds up to 5 blocks, shown as columns.

Footer

Color scheme
The footer's colors. The demo uses the dark Scheme 6.
Show Follow on Shop Checkbox · on
Shows the button only when Shop Pay is turned on in Settings → Payments and the Shop sales channel is installed, and this setting is checked. See Follow on Shop.
Show payment icons Checkbox · on
Shows the icons of the payment methods your store accepts.

Country and language

Show country or region selector Checkbox · on
Shown when your store sells to more than one country or region. Add them under Markets in your Shopify admin.
Show language selector Checkbox · on
Shown when your store has more than one language. Add them under Languages in your Shopify admin.

Menu block

Heading
Uses the menu title when empty.
Menu
The menu to list. New Menu blocks start with your footer menu.

Text block

Heading
The column heading.
Text
A few words about your store.
Link label
Uses the page title when empty. The link is hidden when no page is selected.
Link page
The page the link opens, such as your contact page.

Email signup block

Heading and Text
Shown above the email field. Signups are added to your customer list as subscribed to email marketing.

Social media icons block

Heading
The column heading. The icons are the profiles linked in Theme settings → Social media. When no profile is linked, the whole column (the demo's Follow along) is hidden on the storefront.

Image block

Image
A logo, badge or other image.
Image width 60–360px
How wide the image shows.

Popups

The Popups group at the bottom of the sidebar, shown on every page.

Promo popup

An email signup popup. Signups are added to your customer list as subscribed to email marketing. The popup opens once for each customer. In the theme editor, it shows while this section is selected. To stop showing it, hide or remove the section.

Image
Shows at its own ratio. Landscape and square images fit best.
Heading and Text
The popup's message.
Discount code
Shown only after a successful signup. Create the code in Discounts first; the theme doesn't create or check it.
Delay before opening 0–30s
How long the popup waits after a page loads.
Color scheme
The popup's colors.

Home page sections

Add these with Add section. Most of them also work on other pages, such as product, collection and page templates. Every section has a Color scheme setting, and most have a Subheading and Heading.

Slideshow

Large slides with a label card that holds the text and buttons. Images are never cropped. Each image keeps its own ratio, and its height is capped so the label card shows in the first view: a tall image gets narrower instead of being cut.

Color scheme
The section's colors.
Text layout On image · Below image
Where the label card sits.
Full page width Checkbox · on
Lets the slides reach the edges of the screen. A slide with a tall image gets narrower and is centered.
Change slides automatically Checkbox · off
Slides stop changing once a customer uses a control, and never change for customers who prefer reduced motion.
Change slides every 3–10s
Time between slides.

Slide block (up to 10)

Image
16:9 aspect ratio or wider recommended. The tallest slide sets the height.
Mobile image
Shown on screens under 750px wide. The tallest slide sets the height, so keep its ratio close to your other images.
Video
Plays muted in a loop instead of the image.
Subheading, Heading and Text
The text on the label card.
Button label and Button link
The main button, a ticket stub.
Second button label and Second button link
An optional second button.
Text position Left · Center · Right
Where the label card sits across the slide.
Vertical position Top · Middle · Bottom
Only used when the text shows on the image.
Card color scheme
The colors of the label card.

Lookbook

Image

Show quick add

Before and after

Two images on top of each other with a divider customers drag to compare them. Use two images with the same ratio, taken from the same spot, so the details line up.

Before image and After image
The two images.
Before label and After label
Shown as a sticker on the image. Leave empty to hide it.
Color scheme, Subheading, Heading
The section's colors and heading.
Image ratio Adapt to image · Landscape 3:2 · Square · Portrait 4:5
With Adapt to image, the frame takes the before image's ratio and the after image fills it around its focal point.
Frame width Narrow · Medium · Wide
How wide the frame is.
Divider start position 10–90%
Where the divider sits when the page opens.

Contact page

The contact page template holds the Page section, with its content turned off, and the Contact form section. In your Shopify admin, open the page under Online Store → Pages and pick the contact template.

Field blocks

Phone number: Label, Required
The phone field. One per form.
Text field, Multi-line text, Checkbox: Label, Required
A short answer, a longer answer or a check box. The label also names the answer in the email you receive, so use a different label for each field.
Dropdown: Label, Choices, Required
A list to pick from. Type one choice per line.

Answers to the extra fields arrive in the contact email. Required is checked by the customer's browser only; Shopify itself only checks the email and message.

FAQ page

The faq page template holds the Page section and two Collapsible content sections with example questions. To use it:

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Pages and create or open a page.
  2. Under Theme template, pick faq and save.
  3. In the theme editor, open that page and replace the example rows with your own questions and answers.

Edit the rows as described under Collapsible content. Only keep answers that are true for your store.

Gift card page

The page customers open from their gift card email. It shows the gift card as a ticket: your logo or store name, the balance on a price tag, the expiry date and the code, with a QR code on the stub. Customers can add it to Apple Wallet, copy the code or print it. The page has no settings in the theme editor; it uses your logo and color scheme 1.
