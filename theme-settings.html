Theme settings

Theme settings apply to the whole store. In the theme editor, open Theme settings from the sidebar. The groups and settings below are in the order the editor shows them.

Logo

Logo Image
Your logo in the header. Add alt text in the image picker; without it, the store name is used. Without a logo, the header shows your store name.
Logo width 60–240px · default 140px
Maximum width. Tall and square logos shrink to fit the header height.
Show logo stamp Checkbox · on
Used when no logo is uploaded: a round stamp with the first letters of the first two words of your store name sits next to the store name. The stamp is decoration only.
Favicon Image
The small icon in browser tabs and bookmarks. A 32 x 32px .png is recommended.

Typography

Fonts

Heading font Font · Bricolage Grotesque
The font for headings. Pick any font from the Shopify font library.
Body font Font · Figtree
The font for body text. Its bold, italic and bold italic styles load automatically.
Label font Font · IBM Plex Mono
The font for small labels across the store, such as subheadings, tags, stickers, tickets and receipt lines.
Heading size 80–120% · default 100%
Makes every heading smaller or larger.

Layout

Page width Standard · Wide (default)
The largest width of the page content: Standard is 1240px and Wide is 1440px. Sections with Full page width turned on still reach the edges of the screen.
Page margin 16–48px · default 32px
The space between the content and the edges of the screen. Margin on mobile is always 16px.
Show back to top button Checkbox · on
Shows a button once the page has scrolled past one screen. It takes customers back to the top and moves keyboard focus to the start of the main content.

Colors

Morph comes with seven color schemes. Click a scheme to edit it, or add a new one. Each section picks a scheme with its Color scheme setting. For what each scheme is used for, see Color schemes.

Background
The ground of the section.
Card background
The paper of cards, labels, receipts and tickets.
Card text
Text on the card background.
Text
Main text on the background.
Secondary text
Quieter text, such as subheadings, notes and secondary details.
Borders
Lines and field borders.
Accent background
Colors price tags, sale stickers, ticket stubs and the cart count.
Accent text
Text on the accent background.
Focus ring
Outlines buttons, links and fields selected with the keyboard.
Button background
The fill of buttons.
Button label
The text on buttons.

Tags and buttons

Corner bite size Small · Medium (default) · Large
The size of the round bites cut out of the corners of cards, tags, tickets and buttons.
Tilt tags and stickers Checkbox · on
Price tags and stickers hang at a slight angle. Turn it off to show them straight.

Product cards

These settings apply to product cards everywhere: collection pages, search results, Featured collection, Related products, Recently viewed and other sections.

Image ratio Square (default) · Portrait · Landscape
The shape of the card image.
Image fit Crop (default) · Fit
Crop fills the frame. Fit shows the whole image on a tinted panel.
Subheading Vendor (default) · Product type · SKU · None
The small line shown with the title in the card's label strip.
Show stickers Checkbox · on
Shows one sticker on each card: Sold out first, then your own sticker, then Sale. For your own sticker, add a product tag such as sticker:New (20 characters max).
Show second image on hover Checkbox · on
Shows the product's second media on devices with a mouse.
Show color swatches Checkbox · on
Uses the swatches set on product options. Up to 5 show on each card. A swatch switches the card to that variant, including its image, so attach an image to each variant. See Variant images and swatches.
Show quick add Checkbox · on
Adds a small ticket to each card. Add puts a product with one variant straight into the cart. Options opens the product's options in the card for products with more variants. Sold out products show Sold out.
Show quick view Checkbox · on
A Quick view tag on the card image opens the product's images, videos and buy buttons without leaving the page. Quick view shows the selected variant's image, so attach an image to each variant. It shows no description: View full details leads to the product page.

Animations

Animate page changes Checkbox · on
In browsers that support it, pages fade into each other as customers move around the store. Between two collection pages, product cards that are in both glide to their new place.
Animate tags and buttons on hover Checkbox · on
Motion on hover with a mouse, such as cards lifting and price tags swinging. Turn it off for a calmer store.

Customers who set their device to reduce motion don't see these animations, whatever these settings are.

Cart

Cart type Drawer (default) · Page
Drawer: the cart opens as a receipt over the page, and adding a product opens it. Page: the cart icon and adding a product lead to the cart page.
Show cart note Checkbox · off
Adds a note field to the cart drawer and the cart page, so customers can add instructions to their order.
Gift wrap product Product
Adds an Add gift wrap checkbox to the cart drawer and cart page. Checking it adds this product once, at its own price. Hidden while the product is unavailable.
The product must be available on the Online Store sales channel. To keep it out of search, set the seo.hidden metafield to 1 on it. Gift wrap left alone in the cart is removed automatically. See Gift wrap.

Search

Show search suggestions Checkbox · on
Shows products, collections, pages, articles and query suggestions as customers type.

Social media

Add the full link to each profile. Platforms without a link are hidden. The fields are Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X (Twitter), Pinterest, Snapchat, Tumblr, Vimeo, LinkedIn and Threads.

  • In the footer, the Social media icons block shows the profiles linked here. When no links are set, the block (the demo's Follow along column) is hidden on the storefront.
  • For search engines, the home page automatically adds Organization data: your store name, logo and these social links.
