Getting started

Set up Morph from installation to a published store, then connect the Shopify features the theme uses. Each topic names the exact settings in the theme editor.

Install from the Theme Store

  1. Log in to your Shopify admin, then open the Morph listing in the Shopify Theme Store.
  2. Click Try theme. Morph is added to your theme library as an unpublished theme, so customers don't see it yet.
  3. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes. Morph is listed in the theme library.
Note

Adding a theme never changes your products, collections, orders or customers. Only the look of your online store changes, and only after you publish.

Preview the theme

In Online Store → Themes, click the … (actions) button next to Morph and choose Preview. The preview shows Morph with your own products, collections and pages. To show it to someone else, use Share preview in the preview bar.

Customize in the theme editor

Click Customize next to Morph to open the theme editor.

  • The page selector at the top of the editor switches between the home page, product pages, collection pages, the cart and the other templates.
  • The sidebar lists the header area (Announcement bar and Header), the sections of the page, the footer and the Promo popup. Click a section to edit it, drag sections and blocks to reorder them, and use Add section or Add block to add more. See Sections.
  • Theme settings: open Theme settings from the editor's sidebar to change the logo, fonts, layout, color schemes, product cards, animations, cart, search and social media links. See Theme settings.
  • Save: click Save to keep your changes. Saving an unpublished theme doesn't change your live store.

The Morph home page starts with these sections: Slideshow, Collection list, Featured collection, Promo tiles, Lookbook, Image with text, Featured product, Before and after, Blog posts and Newsletter. Pick your own images, collections, products and blog in each one, or remove the sections you don't need.

The demo store sells home goods, so its texts are written for that shop. Replace the text in the Announcement bar, the Promo popup and the Footer blocks with your own, and only keep claims that are true for your store.

Publish the theme

  1. Go to Online Store → Themes.
  2. Click the … (actions) button next to Morph and choose Publish.
  3. Confirm. Morph becomes your live theme, and your previous theme stays in the theme library so you can switch back.

Menus

Menus are managed in your Shopify admin under Content → Menus. Morph uses three of them by default:

Main menu main-menu
The header navigation on desktop and in the mobile menu. Picked in Header → Menu.
Footer menu footer
The links of the footer's Menu blocks. Each new Menu block starts with this menu, and you can pick another one in its Menu setting.
Customer account menu customer-account-main-menu
The links in the account menu that opens from the account icon in the header. Picked in Header → Customer account menu.

Dropdowns and menu levels

To make a dropdown, drag a menu item onto another item in Content → Menus so it's nested under it. The header shows up to three levels: the items in the header bar (level 1), the links in their dropdown (level 2) and the links under each level 2 link (level 3).

Menu style

In the theme editor, click Header and choose a Menu style:

  • Ticket strip: each dropdown opens as a strip of tickets.
  • Mega menu: shows every link of a dropdown at once in a wide panel. Each level 2 link that has links under it becomes a column headed by a ticket, with its level 3 links hanging under it as tags. Level 2 links without links under them stack together in one last column.

The mobile menu always uses the ticket strip. A collection link with no links under it shows the collection's product count.

Level 3 links that filter a collection

To send customers to part of a collection, for example only the mugs in a kitchen collection, add a collection link and use Filter collection with tags in the menu editor. Only products with that tag are shown, so tag the products first. To filter by product type instead, the product type filter needs to be set up in the Search & Discovery app first (see Filters).

A tag-filtered link shows no product count, because the count would be for the whole collection.

Menu promo

A menu promo adds an image, heading and text to the end of one dropdown. In the theme editor, click Header, then Add block → Menu promo.

  • Menu item: type the exact title of a top-level menu item that has a dropdown, such as Shop. Capital letters and spaces at the ends don't matter. The promo shows at the end of that dropdown and under the item in the mobile menu.
  • One promo per item: add one Menu promo block for each menu item that needs one. If two blocks name the same item, only the first one shows.
  • Image, Heading and Text: a promo with none of them is hidden.
  • Link: the heading links here, and the whole promo becomes clickable. The promo needs a heading for the link to work.

Logo and favicon

Go to Theme settings → Logo.

  • Logo: upload your logo. Add alt text in the image picker; without it, the store name is used.
  • Logo width: the largest width the logo can take. Tall and square logos shrink to fit the header height.
  • Show logo stamp: when no logo is uploaded, the header shows your store name. With this setting on, a round stamp with the first letters of the first two words of your store name sits next to it.
  • Favicon: the small icon in browser tabs. A 32 x 32px .png works best.

Color schemes

Go to Theme settings → Colors. Morph comes with seven color schemes, and every section has a Color scheme setting that picks one of them. Change a scheme once, and every section that uses it changes too.

  • Scheme 1 is the light blue-gray ground most sections use.
  • Scheme 2 is a lighter paper ground.
  • Scheme 3, Scheme 4 and Scheme 5 are blue, green and sand tints. The tint settings of the Collection section use them by default.
  • Scheme 6 is dark ink, used by the Announcement bar and the Footer.
  • Scheme 7 is the orange accent.

Each scheme has these colors:

Background
The ground of the section.
Card background and Card text
The paper of cards, labels, receipts and tickets, and the text on it.
Text and Secondary text
Main text on the ground, and quieter text such as subheadings and notes.
Borders
Lines and field borders.
Accent background and Accent text
The accent colors price tags, sale stickers, ticket stubs and the cart count. Accent text is the text on top of it.
Focus ring
Outlines buttons, links and fields selected with the keyboard.
Button background and Button label
The color of buttons and their text.
Tip

Keep text readable: aim for a contrast of at least 4.5:1 between text and its background, and 3:1 for the focus ring against the background.

Markets: country and language

Morph shows a country or region selector and a language selector in the footer. In the theme editor, click Footer and use:

  • Show country or region selector: shown when your store sells to more than one country or region. Add them in your Shopify admin under Settings → Markets.
  • Show language selector: shown when your store has more than one published language. Add languages under Settings → Languages.

When a customer picks another country or region, prices follow the currency and pricing you set for that market.

Email signup

Morph has four places for email signup. Signups are added to your customer list as subscribed to email marketing.

  • Footer: the Email signup block.
  • Newsletter section: with an Email form block.
  • Promo popup: opens once for each customer after the Delay before opening. Add a Discount code to show it after a successful signup; create the code in Discounts first. In the theme editor, the popup shows while its section is selected.
  • On the password page: Show email signup, with Signup heading and Signup text.

Email fields need a full address with a dot in the domain, such as name@example.com. If the address is incomplete, the form isn't sent and the customer sees a message.

Follow on Shop

The Follow on Shop button lets customers follow your store in the Shop app. In the theme editor, click Footer and check Show Follow on Shop. The button shows only when Shop Pay is turned on in Settings → Payments and the Shop sales channel is installed, and Show Follow on Shop is checked in the Footer.

Local pickup

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Settings → Shipping and delivery and turn on local pickup for a location.
  2. In the theme editor, open a product page, click the Buy buttons block and check Show pickup availability.

The product page then shows a pickup line with the pickup status and ready time at a location, and a button that lists every location with local pickup, with its status, ready time, address and phone number. The line updates when the customer picks another variant.

The pickup line shows only when a location with local pickup has the selected variant in stock.

Unit prices and quantity pricing

Unit prices

When unit pricing is available for your store, turn it on for a product in its pricing details in your Shopify admin and enter the total and base measurement. Morph then shows the unit price with the price on the product page, on product cards and in the cart. There is no theme setting for it.

Quantity rules and volume pricing

Quantity rules (minimum, maximum and increment) and volume pricing come from B2B catalogs. They show only to logged-in B2B customers whose catalog has them. The product page then lists the quantity rules under the buy buttons and a small table of the price breaks, and the total follows the quantity. There is no theme setting for it.

Gift wrap

Morph can add an Add gift wrap checkbox to the cart drawer and the cart page. The gift wrap is a product of your own.

  1. Create a product for gift wrap, such as Gift wrap, with its price and an image.
  2. Make it available on the Online Store sales channel. Without it, the theme can't add it to the cart.
  3. Hide it from search: add a product metafield with the namespace and key seo.hidden and the type Integer (in Settings → Custom data → Products), then set it to 1 on the gift wrap product. Leave the product out of your collections too.
  4. In the theme editor, go to Theme settings → Cart → Gift wrap product and pick the product.
  • Checking the box adds the product once, at its own price. Unchecking it removes the product.
  • The checkbox is hidden while the product is unavailable and while the cart holds nothing else.
  • If a customer removes everything else, gift wrap left alone in the cart is removed automatically.

Variant images and swatches

Variant images

Add all of a product's images first, then open each variant in your Shopify admin and pick its image. Swatches on product cards and quick view need an image attached to each variant: a swatch switches the card to that variant's image, and quick view shows the image of the selected variant.

Color swatches

Morph shows swatches when a product option is connected to the Color category metafield, which holds a color or image for each value.

  1. Open the product in your Shopify admin and choose its Category.
  2. Under Category metafields, click Color and choose Connect to option, then pick the product's Color option (or another color option, such as Glaze).
  3. Check that each option value has its color or image.

Some categories have no Color metafield. Pick a nearby category that has one.

Swatches then show on product cards (Theme settings → Product cards → Show color swatches, up to 5 on each card) and on the product page (Variant picker → Show swatches). On the product page, options with swatches show as swatches even when Picker style is Dropdown.

Filters

Filters on collection and search pages come from Shopify's Search & Discovery app.

  1. Install the Search & Discovery app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. In the app, open Filters and add the filters you want, such as availability, price, product type or vendor.
  3. Option filters such as Size and Color are turned on in the app too. For a Color option connected to the Color category metafield, you can show the filter as swatches.
  4. In the theme editor, check Show filters and Show sorting in the Collection section and the Search section.

Complementary products

Complementary products are items that go well with the product a customer is looking at. Morph shows them in the Complementary products block of the product page, under the heading Goes with by default.

  1. In the Search & Discovery app, open the recommendations for a product and add its complementary products.
  2. In the theme editor, open a product page and check that the Product section has a Complementary products block. Set its Heading and Products to show.

The block shows only when complementary products are set for that product in the Search & Discovery app. Otherwise it stays hidden.
