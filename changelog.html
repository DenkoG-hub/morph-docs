Version 1.0.0

1.0.0

First release.

  • Product cards with price tags, stickers, color swatches, a second image on hover, quick add and quick view.
  • A label-style product page with the Product label block, size chart, trust badges, complementary products, pickup availability, a back in stock form and a sticky add to cart bar.
  • A receipt cart drawer or cart page, with an optional cart note and gift wrap.
  • Ticket-stub buttons, a ticket strip or mega menu with menu promos, and category tags on collection pages.
  • Home page sections including Slideshow, Promo tiles, Lookbook, Before and after, Press receipt and Recently viewed.
  • Seven color schemes, collection tints, motion with a reduced motion path, and country and language selectors.
