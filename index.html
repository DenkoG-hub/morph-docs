Morph theme documentation

Morph is a multipurpose Shopify theme by Denko Themes for small shops that sell physical goods. Its look comes from paper tags, tickets and receipts: prices hang on tags, the product page reads like a label, and the cart prints as a receipt. The demo store sells home goods, and the same sections suit any catalog of physical products.

Get started→ Theme settings

Who Morph is for

  • Shops

    Small shops with physical goods

    Home goods, clothing, accessories, stationery, ceramics, food and gifts. Morph is built around products that customers want to see up close, with a price they can read at a glance.

  • Look

    A designed look without apps

    Tags, tickets and receipts give every page a clear identity. The features on this site come with the theme, so you don't need extra apps to use them. Filters and complementary products are set up in Shopify's Search & Discovery app.

  • Catalogs

    A few products or many collections

    A ticket strip or mega menu with three menu levels, category tags on collection pages, filters, sorting and search suggestions help customers find what they want.

Key features

  • Cards01

    Product cards with price tags

    Each card is a paper ticket with a label strip. The price hangs on a tag over the image, and a sticker shows Sold out, your own text or Sale. Cards can show color swatches, a second image on hover, quick add and quick view.

  • Product page02

    Label-style product page

    The Product label block prints product details like a label, with a details tab and up to four custom tabs. Add a size chart, trust badges under a torn receipt edge, complementary products and a sticky add to cart bar.

  • Cart03

    Receipt cart drawer

    The cart drawer is a receipt that prints from a small countertop printer at the top of the screen. It holds the cart lines, an optional cart note, gift wrap and trust badges. You can use a cart page instead.

  • Buttons04

    Ticket-stub buttons

    Primary buttons are tickets with a stub. When a customer adds a product to the cart, the stub tears off and grows back. Choose the size of the corner bites in Theme settings.

  • Motion05

    Motion with a calm option

    Pages fade into each other in browsers that support it, cards glide between collections, price tags swing on hover and the cart count is stamped when it changes. Customers who prefer reduced motion get a still store, and you can turn motion off.

  • Sections06

    Sections for every page

    Slideshow, Collection list, Featured collection, Promo tiles, Lookbook, Image with text, Featured product, Before and after, Blog posts, Newsletter, Press quotes, Trust badges, Video and more.

Also included

  • Selling in more places Country or region and language selectors in the footer, using your Markets and Languages settings.
  • Customer features Email signup in the footer, a Newsletter section and a Promo popup, plus Follow on Shop and a customer account menu.
  • Product details Unit prices, quantity rules and volume pricing for B2B customers, local pickup availability, a gift card recipient form and a back in stock form.
  • Cart extras A gift wrap checkbox that adds a product you choose, and an optional cart note.
  • Accessibility Keyboard support, a visible focus ring, a skip link and a reduced motion path for every animation.
