Support

Need help with Morph? Check the FAQ first; many answers are there. If you're still stuck, write to us.

Contact us

Send your question through the support form, or email us at denkothemes@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Open the support form→ Email us

What to include

These details help us find the problem quickly:

  • Your store URL, such as http://www.storename.myshopify.com.
  • The theme name and version, as shown in Online Store → Themes.
  • A link to the page where you see the problem.
  • The steps that lead to the problem, and what you expected to happen.
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording.
